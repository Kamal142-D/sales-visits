# Third-party assets

VisitFlow uses icons from the Solar Icon Set by 480 Design, selected from the referenced “7000 FREE UI ICONS” Figma Community collection and used through the SolarIconSetAndroid vector port.

- Solar Icon Set: https://github.com/480-Design/Solar-Icon-Set — CC BY 4.0
- Android port: https://github.com/CMFerrer/SolarIconSetAndroid — MIT
- License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The icons are rendered with VisitFlow colors and sizes; their vector artwork is otherwise unchanged.
